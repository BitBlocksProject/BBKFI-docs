# Tokenomics

## BBKFI Token

<div style={{ display: 'flex', alignItems: 'center', gap: '0.75rem', marginBottom: '1rem' }}>
  <Image src="/images/coins/BBKFI.png" alt="BBKFI" width={48} height={48} />

BBKFI is the primary token of the BitBlocks Finance ecosystem. It serves as both a governance token, 
granting holders voting rights over protocol proposals and a utility token used throughout the platform.

- **Token address:** [`0xfC8E68E30350c6603D3d29fCc8E676380C28FCf4`](https://bscscan.com/token/0xfC8E68E30350c6603D3d29fCc8E676380C28FCf4)
- **Network:** BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID 56)

### Utility

- **Governance** — Vote on DAO proposals to shape the protocol's future.
- **Staking** — Stake BBKFI in earn pools to receive staking rewards.
- **Liquidity** — Provide BBKFI liquidity to earn a share of swap fees.

## How BBKFI is Emitted

BBKFI is not pre-minted. New tokens are created over time by the **MasterChef** contract and distributed to users who stake in the protocol's farms and pools.

- **MasterChef:** [`0xAF7769F4347cc88eD50191eaca7c3ab380097b06`](https://bscscan.com/address/0xAF7769F4347cc88eD50191eaca7c3ab380097b06)

### Fixed emission: 1 BBKFI per block

The protocol emits a fixed **1 BBKFI per block**.

On BNB Smart Chain, blocks happen roughly every 3 seconds, which translates to approximately **~28,800 BBKFI per day** distributed to the community.

<Callout type="info">
  If you stake in a pool, your share of that 1 BBKFI/block depends on (1) the pool's weight relative to other pools, and (2) your share of the tokens staked in that pool. See [Architecture → Staking System (MasterChef)](/contracts/architecture#staking-system-masterchef) for the full technical breakdown.
</Callout>

### Per-pool emission

The 1 BBKFI per block is split across pools according to their weight. The current allocation is:

| Pool | BBKFI per block |
|------|-----------------|
| BBKFI-BNB LP | 0.35 |
| BBKFI-BUSD LP | 0.20 |
| BBKFI-WBBK LP | 0.15 |
| BBKFI staking | 0.10 |
| BNB-BUSD LP | 0.10 |
| WBBK-BNB LP | 0.05 |
| WBBK staking | 0.0025 |
| WBBK-BUSD LP | 0.0025 |

These are the per-block rewards distributed to **each pool as a whole** — your personal rewards depend on your share of that pool. The split is set on-chain via each pool's allocation weight and, like the global rate, can be adjusted through governance.

### Emission is governed by voting

The 1 BBKFI per block figure is the **current** emission rate, not a permanent constant. It can be changed by the protocol's **governance vote**: BBKFI holders can propose and vote to increase or decrease the emission rate over time — for example, to reduce inflation as the ecosystem matures, or to boost rewards during growth phases.

This means tokenomics evolves with the community. Any change to the emission rate is executed on-chain through the governance system, not by any single team member.

## BFIRE Token

<div style={{ display: 'flex', alignItems: 'center', gap: '0.75rem', marginBottom: '1rem' }}>
  <Image src="/images/coins/BFIRE.png" alt="BFIRE" width={48} height={48} />

BFIRE was created as a **liquidity-boosting token for wBBK**. It is an optional, secondary asset used to incentivize wBBK liquidity — it is **not required** to use BitBlocks Finance. Users who do not care about wBBK liquidity incentives can safely ignore it.

- **Token address:** [`0xE5599A12a8fF06A690ffD2790C54A37c74A43208`](https://bscscan.com/token/0xE5599A12a8fF06A690ffD2790C54A37c74A43208)
- **Network:** BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID 56)
- **Total supply:** 1,000,000 BFIRE (fixed)
- **Max transaction:** 100,000 BFIRE

### Transfer fee (1% reflection)

Every BFIRE transfer is charged a **1% reflection fee**, redistributed pro-rata to **all current BFIRE holders** — holding BFIRE passively accrues more BFIRE from every transfer.

The contract and the owner are excluded from the fee to allow internal operations (liquidity injection, pool funding) to run without tax drag.

<Callout type="info">
  The 1% fee applies to **every transfer**, including swaps and transfers between wallets. Plan accordingly when moving BFIRE.
</Callout>
