## SmartChef addresses

The frontend maintains candidate addresses and validates SmartChef shape on-chain instead of treating one static address as the entire system. Each valid pool exposes its own `stakedToken`, `rewardToken`, `rewardPerBlock`, `startBlock`, and `bonusEndBlock`.
