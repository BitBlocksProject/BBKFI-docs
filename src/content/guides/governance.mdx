## Current status

The main application shows an informational Governance screen with a **Coming soon** state. It describes a roadmap for proposals and community participation and links to the separate BitBlocks voting site.

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not interpret a planned workflow as a live control plane. The Finance app does not currently expose buttons to create, queue, execute, or vote on on-chain proposals.
</Callout>

## What BBKFI supports today

<InfoGrid>
  <InfoCard icon="wallet" title="Delegate votes"><p>A holder can call `delegate` to assign voting power to an address. Delegation does not transfer the underlying BBKFI.</p></InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="code" title="Delegate by signature"><p>`delegateBySig` supports typed-data authorization with nonce and expiry validation.</p></InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="layers" title="Current votes"><p>`getCurrentVotes` reads the latest checkpoint for an account.</p></InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="book" title="Prior votes"><p>`getPriorVotes` performs a checkpoint lookup for a finalized block, which is the primitive commonly used for snapshot-based governance.</p></InfoCard>
</InfoGrid>

## Related voting site

The ecosystem exposes [vote.bitblockscrypto.com](https://vote.bitblockscrypto.com/) as a separate product. Verify its domain, connected network, contracts, and wallet prompt independently from the Finance app.

This page will need an update when the main interface ships proposal and voting transactions. Until then, the code-backed statement is simple: **the token has voting primitives; the Finance governance workflow is not live.**
