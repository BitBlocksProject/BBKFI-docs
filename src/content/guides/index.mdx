# Getting Started

Welcome to BitBlocks Finance. This guide will get you set up in a few minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet)
- BNB in your wallet to pay for gas fees on BSC

## Connect Your Wallet

<Steps>

### Open the App

Navigate to [bitblocks.finance](https://bitblocks.finance).

### Click "Connect Wallet"

Click the **Connect Wallet** button in the top-right corner of the interface.

### Select Your Wallet

Choose your wallet provider from the list (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.).

### Switch to BSC

If prompted, approve switching your network to **Binance Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56)**.

### You're In

Your wallet address will appear in the header. You can now access all features.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Always verify you are on the official domain **bitblocks.finance** before connecting your wallet. Bookmark it to avoid phishing sites.
</Callout>
