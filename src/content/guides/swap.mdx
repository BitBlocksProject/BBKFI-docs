# How to Swap

The BitBlocks Swap lets you exchange tokens instantly using the best available route — either the BitBlocks AMM or the 1inch aggregator.

## Step-by-Step

<Steps>

### Go to Swap

Click **Swap** in the left sidebar navigation.

### Select the Tokens

- **From** — Choose the token you want to sell (e.g., BNB).
- **To** — Choose the token you want to receive (e.g., BBKFI).

### Enter an Amount

Type the amount in the **From** field. The estimated output amount will be calculated automatically.

### Review the Quote

Check the price impact and estimated output. The app shows:
- Exchange rate
- Price impact
- Minimum received (accounting for slippage tolerance)

### Approve (First Time Only)

If this is your first time swapping a token, you will need to approve the router contract to spend it. Click **Approve [TOKEN]** and confirm in your wallet.

### Confirm the Swap

Click **Swap** and confirm the transaction in your wallet. Once the transaction is mined, your new tokens will appear in your wallet.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  **Routing**: By default, swaps route through the BitBlocks Router. For large trades, the app may automatically use the 1inch aggregator for a better price.
</Callout>

## Slippage Tolerance

Slippage is the difference between the expected price and the actual execution price. You can adjust slippage tolerance in the swap settings (gear icon). The default is **0.5%**.

| Slippage | Use Case |
|----------|----------|
| 0.1% | Stable pairs with deep liquidity |
| 0.5% | Default — most tokens |
| 1–5% | Low liquidity tokens or volatile markets |
