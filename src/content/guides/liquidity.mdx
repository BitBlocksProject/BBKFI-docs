## V3 or V2?

| | BitBlocks V3 | BitBlocks V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Position | NFT with its own price range | Fungible LP token (ERC-20) |
| Liquidity | Concentrated between a min and max price | Spread across every price |
| Swap fee | Pool tier: 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.3% or 1% | 0.25% per hop |
| Fees | Accrue separately; collected on demand | Added to pool reserves |
| Farming | Not farmable in the app yet | Eligible pairs can be staked in MasterChef |
| Effort | Out-of-range positions stop earning and need attention | Passive |

**Create position** opens V3 by default. A **V3 / V2** switch in the page header selects the classic flow. The V3 guide is [Concentrated liquidity (V3)](/guides/concentrated-liquidity); the rest of this page covers the shared screens and V2.

Pools under US$ 10 of TVL are hidden unless you hold liquidity in them, search for them, or show small pools. A row is tagged when your wallet has V3 liquidity, V2 LP, or a farm stake in that pool.

### How pool APR is calculated

Pool data comes from on-chain snapshots taken about every five minutes by the BitBlocks Finance info backend.

- **Fee APR** annualizes the LP share of fees from the last 24 hours against TVL. When 24 hours of data are missing, it falls back to 7-day fees; a new pool shows that it is still settling.
- **V2 farm APR** is added for pairs with an active MasterChef farm, and only applies to LP that is staked.
- **V3 position APR** scales the pool's fees by the share of active liquidity your position provides, so an out-of-range position earns no fees.

APR is an observation of recent activity, not a promised rate.

## Add V2 liquidity

<Steps>

### Choose Create position, then V2

Select **Create position** in the sidebar and switch the header toggle to **V2**, or follow a V2 link from a pool or farm.

### Select both assets

Choose two different tokens. Native BNB is represented by WBNB inside the pair while the router handles wrapping during supply. Equivalent BNB/WBNB pairs are rejected.

### Enter one amount

For an existing pair, the other amount is derived from current reserves. For a new pair with no reserves, the first provider chooses the initial price ratio. Supply is blocked when an amount exceeds the wallet balance.

### Review the preview

Check both amounts, estimated pool share, LP output, and minimum accepted amounts. Slippage protection is applied to the supply call.

### Approve ERC-20 assets

Approve each ERC-20 that the BitBlocks Router cannot already spend. Native BNB has no ERC-20 approval step.

### Supply

Confirm the transaction. The factory creates the pair if it does not exist, and the pair mints LP tokens to the connected address.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  On the first mint, the pair permanently locks `MINIMUM_LIQUIDITY` to the zero address. This is standard V2 behavior and prevents the pool from being completely drained by burning all LP supply.
</Callout>

### V2 fees

The pair charges 0.25% on each swap input. The factory's `feeTo` is set, so the pair mints protocol LP tokens worth 8/25 of fee growth whenever liquidity is added or removed. Liquidity providers keep about **0.17%** of each trade.

## Stake or remove V2 liquidity

From **My positions**, a V2 row shows LP in the wallet and LP staked in a MasterChef farm. An amber alert flags LP left unstaked in a pair that has a live farm. Select **Stake** in the row's rewards cell, or **Stake LP** in the alert, to deposit wallet LP in its farm without leaving the page.

<Steps>

### Open the Manage menu

Choose **Unstake LP** to return staked LP to the wallet, or **Remove liquidity**. **Add liquidity** opens the V2 add flow for the same pair.

### Choose what to remove

For staked LP, choose **Unstake only** to return LP to the wallet, or **Unstake & remove** to withdraw from the farm and remove liquidity. Unstaking also pays out pending BBKFI.

### Authorize the LP token

BitBlocks LP tokens support EIP-712 permit. The interface first offers a signature-based authorization and falls back to a normal on-chain approval if needed.

### Confirm removal

The router burns the LP tokens and transfers the proportional reserves back to the wallet, subject to minimum-amount protection and deadline.

</Steps>

## Risks to evaluate

- **Impermanent loss:** divergence between the two assets can outweigh fee income, in V2 and V3 alike.
- **Low liquidity:** small reserves increase price impact and can make exit values volatile.
- **Token behavior:** fee-on-transfer, rebasing, pausable, or malicious tokens can behave differently from a standard ERC-20.
- **Pool creation:** anyone can create a pair or a V3 pool. A pool's existence is not an endorsement of either token.
- **Staked LP tokens:** LP deposited in a farm will not appear as a freely available wallet balance until withdrawn.

<Callout type="warning">
  Never supply at an unintended initial ratio. In a new pool, bots can immediately arbitrage a price that differs from the wider market.
</Callout>
